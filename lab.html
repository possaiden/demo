Use layer, part 1

The Business AI Stack: five core categories

Each category suits a different kind of work. Pick the one that matches what the task really needs, and combine them when one alone isn't enough.

Choose the least complex tool that meets the task's accuracy, control, cost, speed and compliance needs. Start simple, and add complexity only when the task needs it.

Complexity and governance increase as you go up the stack

More capability brings more setup, more cost and a greater need for oversight.

Systems beat tools

A reliable AI solution usually combines these parts rather than relying on one tool.

Knowledge sourceVerified documents and data.
Reasoning layerDrafts, explains and analyses.
Workflow stepRuns the process reliably.
Human approvalA named person signs off on what matters.

Your tool decision should also reflect the business function, impact, risk, governance and human oversight.
